AI-assisted loan file preparation

SBA 7(a) first. A working demo on synthetic data.

The tool prepares the file. The underwriter decides.

Status: local demo. No real borrower data, no real lender system, nothing deployed.

The problem we are testing

  • Commercial loan files are document-heavy: read statements, key figures, cross-check, apply program rules, write the memo.
  • That work sits before the judgment call, and the judgment call is what experienced underwriters are for.
  • Hypothesis: prepare the file with sources attached, so underwriters spend their time deciding. Unproven until measured.

What it might be worth

ESTIMATE ONLY. Durations are our guesses, not measurements.

  • Modeled assisted-to-manual time ratio: 0.19 to 0.50 at middle assumptions.
  • At pessimistic assumptions the ratio exceeds 1.0 (no saving).
  • We make no savings claim. The first 30 days measure the real manual baseline with two of your underwriters.

Controls built in

  • Human decides: enforced in the database; only an underwriter acting as themselves can record a decision.
  • Evidence: no fact without a page and quote; accepted means faithfully extracted, not verified true.
  • Isolation: per-lender data separation enforced by the database; append-only, hash-chained audit log.
  • Safety: identifiers encrypted and redacted before any model call; document text is never treated as instructions.
  • Sign-in and access: OpenID Connect, role-based access (only underwriters decide), admin screens for users and roles, sessions revocable at once, every sign-in and admin change audited.
  • Hardening: rate limits, scrubbed logs, security headers, threat model mapped to tests.

What it is not yet

  • No real model, OCR, identity provider or hosting; no real documents tested. Sign-in is built to the OpenID Connect standard and tested with a stand-in provider.
  • Debt service covers the new loan only, with a placeholder rate; eligibility, collateral and credit reports are not evaluated.
  • No third-party security test, no lender vendor-risk review, and items awaiting counsel and compliance review.
