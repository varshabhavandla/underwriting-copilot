## How to read this report
- **Measured**: results of running the real pipeline (database, validators, rules, memo verifier) on seeded synthetic packets with known ground truth.
- **Estimated**: the time comparison in section 4 is a model with assumed per-action durations. It is not a measurement of real underwriters and must not be quoted as a result.
- **Not measured**: real model accuracy, real OCR, real documents, real underwriter time. The mock provider reads printed labels, so extraction scores here validate the verification, validator, rule and memo layers, not a language model.

## 7. Limitations and next steps
- Synthetic documents are generated from the same templates the mock reads; real documents are messier. Real accuracy is unknown until a real provider runs on this harness (B-124) and on redacted real samples with consent.
- Truth for the time model is not human timing. A timed manual baseline by at least two people, and instrumented console timings, are required before any time-savings statement.
- Classes not covered: collateral shortfall (collateral data is not collected), and documents the rule pack lists as not evaluated.
- The set is pilot-sized; report ranges, not point claims.
